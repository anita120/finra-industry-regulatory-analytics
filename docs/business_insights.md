# Business Insights

## FINRA Industry Regulatory Analytics

### Analysis Period: 2011–2021

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This project analyzes FINRA industry registration data from 2011 through 2021 to understand how reported registration categories changed over time.

The analysis shows two distinct directional patterns:

- Reported broker-dealer-related categories declined over the period.
- Investment Adviser Firms-Only and Securities Industry Registered Firms increased over the same period.

The number of firms in the **All FINRA-Registered Broker-Dealer Firms at Year End** category declined from **4,455 in 2011 to 3,394 in 2021**, representing a **23.82% decrease**.

In contrast, **Investment Adviser Firms-Only** increased from **25,524 to 31,669**, a **24.08% increase**, while **Securities Industry Registered Firms** increased from **29,979 to 35,063**, a **16.96% increase**.

These findings describe historical changes in the reported registration categories. They should not be interpreted as evidence of the causes of those changes.

# 17. Data Limitations and Considerations

The following limitations should be considered when interpreting the results:

1. The analysis covers **2011–2021** historical observations.
2. The API returned overlapping historical snapshots with different report dates.
3. A consistent snapshot-selection rule was applied to construct the analytical series.
4. Registration categories may overlap and should not be treated as mutually exclusive.
5. Category totals should not be interpreted as unique firm counts.
6. Category shares represent reported category observations rather than market share.
7. Correlation results describe statistical relationships and do not establish causality.
8. The analysis identifies historical patterns but does not independently determine the regulatory, economic, or business causes behind those changes.
